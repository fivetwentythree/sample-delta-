# A Place to Begin

The morning leaves a little light  
along the windowsill,  
and all the things we meant to do  
are waiting for us still.  

No need to make a mountain move  
or teach the stars to stay;  
just set a cup beside a friend  
and ask about their day.  

The garden grows by quiet work,  
by rain we never hear;  
the roots reach out beneath our feet  
while nothing new appears.  

So leave some room upon the page,  
some bread beneath the crust;  
not everything we hope to build  
requires a plan—just trust.  

By evening, let the small things count:  
a hinge that swings again,  
a sentence found, a window raised  
to let the weather in.
